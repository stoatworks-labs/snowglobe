Photosensitivity warning

With Shake and Count both at full, the snow can flash. Thousands of white flakes thrown up on every kick or beat can cover most of the globe and then fall away, so a dark clip behind them goes white and dark again. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, keep Shake and Count below full.

Broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom's, and WCAG's) puts the line at three flashes in any second, six rises and falls in brightness. Measured from the plugin's own output (the whole frame and each third of it, at 60 fps): over Resolume's 33 demo clips with music through the plugin's audio path, the defaults stayed under the line on every clip (worst 5); at full Shake and Count, under Kick or Beat, one dark clip went over it, 7 to 8 rises and falls in its worst second over a third of the picture. This page's own track over the harness's colour card stayed under the line at the defaults (0) and at full Shake and Count (1). The page opens at the plugin's defaults. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
